## 🎙️ **Special Features**

### Voice Commands
- Click 🎤 **Mic icon** to speak your question

1. **⚡ Technical** - Detailed data
2. **📊 Standard** - Balanced info
3. **👥 Public** - Simple language
4. **🚨 Emergency** - Critical only

### Location Features
- Auto-detects your location for predictions
- Provides localized weather forecasts
- Shows nearest monitoring stations
